How to Buy $PROC - The Easiest Guide

Follow these simple steps to buy PROC, even if you’ve never used crypto before.

Step 1: Install MetaMask

First, install MetaMask, your secure crypto wallet. Available for Chrome, Firefox, and mobile.

Download MetaMask

Step 2: Buy BNB Directly in MetaMask

You don’t need to sign up for an exchange! Buy BNB inside MetaMask with a debit/credit card:

  1. Open MetaMask and select Binance Smart Chain (BSC Mainnet) from the top-left network selector.
  2. Click "Buy & Sell" (or "Buy" on mobile).
  3. Choose a payment provider and follow the instructions.
  4. Once purchased, BNB will appear in your MetaMask wallet.
Buy BNB Now

Step 3: Swap BNB for PROC on PancakeSwap

Now that you have BNB, swap it for PROC using PancakeSwap:

  1. Go to PancakeSwap.
  2. Click "Connect Wallet" and select MetaMask.
  3. Set the output currency to PROC (or paste this contract: 0x372dC085148adDeA482671473BFa97a27DEA7081).
  4. Enter the amount of BNB you want to swap.
  5. Set slippage to 15-20%, then click "Swap".
Buy PROC on PancakeSwap

Step 4: Add PROC to MetaMask

After buying PROC, add it to MetaMask:

  1. Open MetaMask and go to "Assets".
  2. Click "Import Tokens".
  3. Paste the PROC contract address: 0x372dC085148adDeA482671473BFa97a27DEA7081.
  4. Click "Add Custom Token".

Now, you’ll see your PROC balance in MetaMask! 🎉
