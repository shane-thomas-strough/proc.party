$PROC Whitepaper – Earn While You Wait

Stay patient and prosper—let mathematics and strategy fuel your gains.

1. Executive Summary

ProcrastinationCoin ($PROC) is a deflationary, community-driven token on Binance Smart Chain (BSC) designed to reward patience through reflection, controlled burns, and a two-phase tax structure. Inspired by the Fibonacci sequence and π (pi), PROC’s tokenomics incorporate mathematical elegance while remaining accessible and engaging for both investors and gamers.

Key Features:

  • Deflationary Tokenomics: Ongoing burns (linked to pi-threshold logic) steadily reduce supply.
  • Two-Phase Tax: Launch phase (16% total) automatically shifts to a Sustain phase (10% total) after ~60 days—or earlier if forced—ensuring adaptability and longevity.
  • Reflections: A portion of every transaction is redistributed to holders, boosting wallet balances over time.
  • Community-Driven Ecosystem: Mini-games, staking, and consistent marketing feed both project growth and token utility.
  • Fair Launch Model: No presale, with liquidity added directly on PancakeSwap.

2. Tokenomics & Supply

Total Supply: 1,618,033,988,000 PROC (derived from the Fibonacci sequence).

Reflections & Burns: Reflection fees automatically reward holders in proportion to their holdings, and the burn mechanism removes tokens from circulation permanently.

Phase 1 (Launch Fees: 16% total)

  • 4% Reflection – Redistributed to all holders.
  • 2% Burn – Permanently removes tokens from circulation.
  • 3% Liquidity – Builds liquidity, promoting price stability.
  • 3% Marketing – Fuels awareness campaigns, listings, and strategic partnerships.
  • 2% Development – Funds ongoing improvements, audits, and new features.
  • 2% Gaming – Supports community rewards, prize pools, and staking incentives.

Phase 2 (Sustain Fees: 10% total)

  • Activates automatically ~60 days after launch (or earlier via the forceSustainPhase() function in the contract).
  • 4% Reflection
  • 1% Burn
  • 2% Liquidity
  • 1% Marketing
  • 1% Development
  • 1% Gaming

Note on Pi-Threshold: If the total supply ever drops below a pi-based threshold (piThreshold in the contract), the burn allocation is redirected to reflections, accelerating rewards for holders once supply is sufficiently reduced.

3. Roadmap

PROC’s roadmap is milestone-driven rather than rigidly time-bound. Major phases include:

Phase 1: Launch & Liquidity

  • Token deployment and PancakeSwap liquidity addition.
  • Begin with 16% launch fees to fund marketing, development, and liquidity.
  • Kickoff marketing campaigns and community-building (Telegram, Twitter, Discord).

Phase 2: Sustain Transition

  • Automatic fee adjustment to 10% after ~60 days (or earlier via forced sustain).
  • Introduce staking (Idle Yield) and initial community mini-games.
  • Ramp up partnerships and influencer collaborations.

Phase 3: Ecosystem Expansion

  • Release additional games that are meme-friendly and reward-focused.
  • Undergo a CertiK (or equivalent) audit for heightened transparency.
  • Initiate DAO-style governance discussions for further tax changes.

Phase 4: Cross-Chain Growth & Sustainability

  • Explore bridges to other networks (e.g., Polygon, Ethereum).
  • Potential CEX listings once the project meets volume requirements.
  • Ongoing marketing and community-driven expansions.

4. Utility & Community Engagement

  • Reflections: Passive yield for holding.
  • Mini-Games & Gamification: Sloth-themed mini-games (roulette, poker, etc.) drive organic demand for PROC to play and earn.
  • Future Staking: “Idle Yield” mechanics reward users for simply holding or staking.
  • Organic Meme Culture: Sloth-themed memes and social media challenges boost community spirit, encouraging holders to spread the word and drive healthy FOMO.

5. Security & Transparency

  • Contract Code Verified: The PROC smart contract is public and verifiable.
  • Time-Based Fee Logic: The shift from Launch fees to Sustain fees is automated by a timestamp check in _getFeeRates().
  • Liquidity Locked: A significant portion of liquidity is locked to enhance confidence.
  • Ownership Controls: The owner can adjust fees (up to a maximum of 25%) and force the Sustain phase early, but cannot exceed these security-limited parameters.
  • Audits: Third-party security audits are planned for major updates.
