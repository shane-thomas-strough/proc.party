What is $PROC?

$PROC is a deflationary token on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC) that rewards holders through reflections and offers a playful “slothverse” of mini-games. Essentially, you can earn while you wait—the perfect token for those who want to hold or those who love crypto-themed fun.

Quick Start: How to Buy $PROC

  1. Install a BSC-compatible wallet (e.g., MetaMask).
  2. Switch your wallet network to Binance Smart Chain.
  3. Go to PancakeSwap and import $PROC’s contract address.
  4. Swap BNB for $PROC (set slippage ~20% at launch).
